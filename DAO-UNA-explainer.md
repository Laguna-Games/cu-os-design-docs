# CU DAO UNA Explainer
As the Crypto Unicorns DAO transitions to full community governance, we want to provide this brief explainer on the Unincorporated Nonprofit Association (UNA) structure that serves as the “legal wrapper” for the DAO. The UNA provides a legal framework that safeguards members and ensures the DAO's operations align with its mission.

#### Understanding the UNA:
A UNA is a legal entity designed for groups operating without profit motives. It offers a legally-recognized structure for organizations like the Crypto Unicorns DAO, enabling them to enter contracts, hold assets, and engage in legal activities while protecting individual members from personal liability. The UNA can hold property, manage assets, and enter into contracts on behalf of the DAO. The UNA implements decisions made by tokenholders through DAO governance processes. Whether it’s funding a project, entering into contracts, or managing intellectual property, the UNA ensures these actions are legally binding and operationally sound.

#### The UNA's Role in the Crypto Unicorns Ecosystem:
Within the Crypto Unicorns ecosystem, the UNA serves as the legal representative of the DAO. It holds and manages the intellectual property and the treasury, and enters into agreements on behalf of the DAO. This structure allows the community to focus on governance and development, knowing that the UNA handles legal and administrative responsibilities. The UNA’s legal status allows the DAO to establish partnerships, negotiate agreements, and engage with external stakeholders. The DAO is therefore empowered replace any function currently held by Laguna Games at any time.

#### Legal Protections Afforded by the UNA:
The UNA structure is crucial for limiting personal liability. A recent ruling involving Lido DAO highlighted the risks of operating without a legal entity. A California federal judge determined that Lido DAO (which was “unwrapped,” in the sense that it did not have a legal entity like a UNA) functioned as a general partnership, exposing individual token holders who participated in governance to personal liability for the DAO's actions.

The Crypto Unicorns DAO has been structured as a UNA with the intention of shielding individual from such liability simply by virtue of holding CU tokens or voting on DAO initiatives.

#### Transitioning Governance to the Community:
Laguna Games is committed to transferring control of the DAO to the community. The UNA will serve as the vehicle for this transition, with community members taking on roles within the UNA to guide the DAO's future. This approach ensures that governance remains decentralized and community-driven, with the UNA providing the necessary legal framework.

The UNA owns the artistic and creative intellectual property associated with the Crypto Unicorns game, including characters, storylines, and visual assets. While Laguna Games retains ownership of the underlying software and technology, it has licensed this to the UNA. This ensures that the DAO can continue developing and managing the game without interruption.

#### The Role of the Charter in DAO Governance:
The DAO's charter is a foundational document outlining its mission, governance structure, and operational procedures. It serves as a guide for decision-making and ensures that all actions align with the DAO's objectives. The charter also defines the relationship between the DAO and the UNA, clarifying roles and responsibilities to maintain transparency and accountability. Importantly, as a nonprofit entity, the UNA cannot distribute profits to members. All funds must be reinvested in the DAO’s ecosystem, such as game enhancements, marketing, and community initiatives.
